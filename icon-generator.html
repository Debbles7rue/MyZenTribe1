📱 How to Install on Your Phone:

  1. Download all icon sizes and place them in your public/icons/ folder
  2. Add the manifest.json file to your public/ folder
  3. Add the meta tags to your layout file
  4. Deploy to Netlify
  5. On iPhone: Visit your site in Safari → tap Share button → "Add to Home Screen"
  6. On Android: Visit in Chrome → tap menu → "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App"
